## The Problem

Every creator, gamer, and student has **10+ online profiles** scattered across platforms. Sharing them means sending a messy list of URLs. Bio-link tools exist — but they're **boring, generic, and expensive**.

**Linktree** charges $6-30/month. **HiHello** targets corporates. **Bento** shut down. None of them speak the visual language of Gen Z India — the gamers, anime fans, memers, and digital artists aged 16-25.

## The Solution

**MiniYou** — a cyberpunk-themed digital identity card that lives at `miniyou.co.in/yourname`.

One link. A holographic card with 3D tilt effects. All your socials. Shareable in seconds — online or in person via QR.

## The Market

**Target:** Indian Gen Z (ages 16-25) — gamers, anime fans, content creators, digital artists, college students.

**Market size:** 400M+ Indians aged 15-29. 350M+ active social media users. Every one of them shares links.

**Why now:**
- Digital identity is becoming a necessity, not a luxury
- India's creator economy is booming (100M+ creators by 2025)
- No player is serving the aesthetic-first, mobile-first Indian youth market
- Bento.me (the design-forward competitor) shut down in Feb 2026 — gap in market

**Pricing advantage:** At Rs 99/month, MiniYou is **5-30x cheaper** than every global competitor while being built specifically for the Indian market.
